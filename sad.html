If you're sad :c

Sometimes, life feels overwhelming and we can't seem to find our way through the fog. It's okay to feel lost or sad; these emotions are a part of being human. But don't let them define you. Remember, the pain you're feeling now is not permanent. It's just a chapter in your journey, and this chapter will eventually end. Even when everything feels like it's falling apart, trust that you are being shaped into a stronger version of yourself. You have faced challenges before, and you've always found a way to rise above them. This time will be no different. Keep going, take it one day at a time, and know that your resilience is more powerful than you think. You will heal, and you will emerge stronger. Better days are ahead—keep believing in them, and most importantly, keep believing in yourself.
