If you're angry

I know you're angry right now, and that's okay. Anger is a natural emotion—it means something matters to you. Don't feel guilty for feeling this way. What's important is what you choose to do with that anger. You don't have to suppress it, but try not to let it control you either. Take a deep breath, step back if you need to, and allow yourself the space to process things. Sometimes, clarity comes not from reacting, but from pausing. You are stronger than this moment, and you have the power to respond with calm, not just reaction. Whatever it is, it doesn't define you. You are more than this emotion, and you deserve peace. Give yourself the grace to feel, and the wisdom to heal.
